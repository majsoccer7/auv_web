About us

Building a better robot every year.

We are part of Women in Robotics, Engineering, and Development (WIRED). Our purpose is to build an autonomous underwater vehicle for the annual RoboSub competition and to compete in events such as NURC. Each year, past experience and new ideas come together to construct a better robot than the year before.

Members range from first-years to seniors and come from many engineering majors. No previous experience is necessary. Membership is open to all undergraduate engineering students.

Our mission

Classroom knowledge, real water, real stakes.

The underwater robotics team gives students a place to apply coursework to a working vehicle. Members learn engineering skills, communication, and how to work on a multidisciplinary team. Building an AUV or ROV forces creative problem-solving and grows leaders.

Subteams

Where you can plug in.

Mechanical

Build an efficient, watertight vehicle from hydrodynamics, structures, and manufacturing.

Electrical

Design power distribution, sensors, and the electronics that keep the vehicle alive underwater.

Programming

Deploy thruster control and computer vision on the vehicle’s onboard computer.
