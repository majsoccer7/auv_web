The first WIRED / WE REAF competition vehicle.

Mechanical

  • Four thrusters on an outer frame
  • Main hull with watertight endcaps for electronics
  • Miniature hulls on top arches for batteries and camera
  • 3D-printed kill-switch housing

Electrical

  • Two 14.8V 10,000 mAh LiPo batteries
  • VN-100 AHRS donated by VectorNav
  • Two Measurement Specialties US-300 pressure sensors
  • Two SS-Aquacam cameras
  • Temperature sensor to protect electronics

Programming

  • OpenCV on camera video
  • Two Raspberry Pi 2s for image processing and algorithms
  • Four Arduino Unos between sensors and the Pis
