Competition results, outreach, and campus showcases.

August 6, 2023

RoboSub 2023 semifinalists

Eleven engineering students from Texas A&M competed against 33 other teams in San Diego. A year of work paid off: the team reached the semifinals.

August 2, 2022

Brightest Spark of Curiosity — RoboSub 2021

The team was recognized for asking questions and learning from peers: how other teams were structured, and the ins and outs of their AUVs.

August 4, 2019

2nd place video, RoboSub 2019

A vampire-themed movie trailer introduced the team. They finished one point from first.

May 5, 2018

Women in Engineering program for Girl Scouts

WE REAF traveled to the STEM center grand opening in Dallas to talk with Girl Scouts about the AUV team and opportunities for women in STEM.

Engineering Project Showcase

WE REAF joined 200+ College of Engineering teams presenting to industry. Nessie was displayed in public for the first time.

Student Research Week

The team presented Nessie’s specs and year-over-year improvements in poster form at the nation’s largest student-run research symposium.

1st place, Virtual Project Showcase (non-capstone)
