FAQ

Common questions about joining WIRED Underwater Robotics at Texas A&M.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Members of all experience levels are welcome.

What is the time commitment?

About three hours per week: two in a subteam meeting and one in a general meeting. Members can join more than one subteam.

What opportunities does this team bring?

Connections to industry, technical experience, mentorship, and a support system — plus a competition vehicle you helped build.

Can graduate students join?

Typically graduate students mentor from the outside. Membership can be reviewed case by case.

How do I join?

Show up to meetings. Times are listed on the home and join pages. More information is sent through Discord. Email reaf.tamu@gmail.com to be added.
